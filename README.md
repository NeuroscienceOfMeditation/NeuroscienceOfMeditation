## The Quiet Mind: a free film course

Seven short, evidence-checked films on what meditation, breathing and sleep do to the brain. Free, no sign-up, with captions, transcripts and sources for every claim.

- Words, sources, practices and transcripts: `_data/free_course.yml`
- Lesson page design: `_layouts/film-lesson.html`
- Course home page: `free-course.html`
- Film, poster and caption files: `films/` (named after each lesson's slug)
