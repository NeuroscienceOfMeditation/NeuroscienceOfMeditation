How these films are made

Each film starts from the published studies, not from summaries of them. The numbers are checked against the papers, the limits of each study are said out loud (a mouse study is called a mouse study), and simplified diagrams are labelled as simplified.

Want to go deeper?

The films are a starting point. The full courses go further, with practice, and the articles take one claim at a time.
