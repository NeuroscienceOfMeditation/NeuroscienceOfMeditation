Ordering

How ordering works

There is no checkout to get lost in. You tell us what you'd like, and a person replies with everything you need.

  1. Choose a course or book

    Press Join or Order copy on Courses & Books. The contact form opens with your choice already filled in.

  2. Send the form

    Add your name and email and send it. Prefer your own email? Write to adiyogistudios@gmail.com instead.

  3. Get a reply within two working days

    We send the price, how to pay, and for a course the next start date and how the sessions run, so you know exactly what you're joining before you pay. All sales are final, with no refunds.

  4. Receive your copy or your place

    Books are digital copies; once payment arrives you get the download details by email. Course places are confirmed by email.

Good to know

  • Prices on the site are in Indian rupees.
  • All sales are final: we don't offer refunds on courses or books. That's why you get the price, dates and format before you pay, so ask us anything first.
  • Changed your mind about a book? Swap it for another one in your order any time before download.
  • Aṇūṭṭara, our magazine, and the Swar Yoga tool are free and need no order.
  • Questions about an order: write to adiyogistudios@gmail.com with your name and what you ordered.
