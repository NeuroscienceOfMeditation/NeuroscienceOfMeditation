# The mailing list: how it works

This folder is for you, not the website. Anything in a folder whose name starts
with `_` stays off the site, so the drafts here are private.

- `README.md`: this guide
- `welcome-email.md`: the email new subscribers get when they confirm
- `2026-09-30-recognition-as-liberation.md`: the email for the new article, ready to paste
- `import-template.csv`: the layout Kit needs to add people you already know, in groups

## What's already set up

Everyone who signs up on the site joins one list in Kit (kit.com), form 9927031:

- the signup card at the end of every article
- the Newsletter button and popup on every page
- the new page **neuroscienceofmeditation.in/newsletter.html** (share this one on
  Instagram and WhatsApp)

Kit sends each new person a confirmation email first. Only people who click it
are added, so nobody ends up on the list without asking.

The site also has a feed at **/feed.xml** that updates itself with every new
article, for people who use feed readers.

## Sending an email for each new article

Kit's free plan sends emails by hand (a "broadcast"). Sending them automatically
from the feed is a paid feature, so for now:

1. Publish the article on the site and open it once to check it looks right.
2. In Kit, go to **Send → Broadcasts → New broadcast**.
3. Paste the subject, preview text and body from the draft in this folder (there
   is one for "Recognition as Liberation"; copy its layout for later articles).
4. Under recipients, choose **All subscribers**, or a group (see below).
5. Send yourself a test email first, then send or schedule it.

Morning (7 to 9 am India time) tends to be a good time to send.

## Groups: tags and segments

Kit doesn't have separate lists. Instead, you label people with **tags** and
choose tags when you send.

| Tag | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| `website` | Signed up on the site (you can set form 9927031 to add this in Kit) |
| `students` | People who took a class with you |
| `swar-yoga-course` | People who joined Swar Yoga, properly |
| `workshop-2026` | People from a particular workshop or event |
| `philosophy` | Readers who asked for the philosophy pieces |
| `research` | Readers who asked for the research pieces |

To send to one group: in the broadcast's recipients, pick **Matching any of
these tags** and choose the tag. To send to a combination that you use often
(for example students *and* course people), save it as a **segment** under
**Grow → Subscribers → Segments**.

## Adding people you already know

You can add people who have **agreed to hear from you**: your students, workshop
participants who ticked a box, people who asked to be kept posted.

1. Open `import-template.csv` in Excel or Google Sheets.
2. Put one person per row: `email`, `first_name` (optional) and `tags`. Several
   tags go in one cell separated by commas, like `students,swar-yoga-course`.
3. Delete the three example rows, then save as CSV.
4. In Kit, go to **Grow → Subscribers → Add subscribers → Import a CSV**, upload
   the file and match the columns (Kit asks which column is which).

Kit adds the tags from the file to everyone in it. Anyone who is already on the
list keeps their existing tags.

Tip: collect new emails with a sign-up sheet or a Google Form at classes and
workshops, with a line such as "Send me new articles and course dates", so you
always know who agreed.

## Never import a list you found, bought or copied

Don't upload emails from lists sold online, collected from websites, or copied
from WhatsApp groups or Instagram followers who never asked for email.

- **It's against Kit's rules.** Kit can close the account, and with it every
  real subscriber you've gathered.
- **It hurts delivery.** People who didn't ask mark the email as spam, and then
  your emails start going to spam for your real readers too.
- **It's against the law in India.** The Digital Personal Data Protection Act,
  2023 requires a person's consent before you use their email address this way.

The good news: a list of a few hundred people who asked to hear from you gets
far more readers than a bought list of thousands.

## Growing the list the right way

- Put the newsletter link (neuroscienceofmeditation.in/newsletter.html) in your
  Instagram bio, and mention it when you post an article.
- At the end of a class, invite people to sign up on the page themselves.
- Share each new article in your WhatsApp groups with the article link. People
  who want more can subscribe from the page.
- Each Aṇūṭṭara issue ends with the signup card.

## One thing to set in Kit

Kit needs a postal address at the bottom of every email (a legal requirement in
many countries). Add one under **Settings → Email** in Kit. A studio address or
a PO box is fine.
