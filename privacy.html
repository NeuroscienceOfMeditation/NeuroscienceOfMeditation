Privacy notice

What this site collects, why, where it's kept, and how to download or delete it. Written to be read, not skimmed past.

Version 1.1 · 17 September 2026

Who we are

Neuroscience of Meditation is run by Aditi, a doctoral fellow in the science of yoga, based in Delhi, India. For any question about your data, use the contact page.

1. The newsletter

What we collect: your email address, and only if you subscribe.

Why: to send you evidence-checked articles and practices. That's all it is used for.

Where it's kept: with Kit (kit.com), the service that sends our emails.

Your choices: every email has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops all emails straight away.

2. The Swara open record

Only if you tick both boxes and choose Join the open record on the Swar Yoga page. The tool works fully without joining.

What we collect for each observation:

  • which side you report is flowing, and what the Svarodaya rules predicted;
  • the time of the observation, and minutes since sunrise;
  • your location rounded to about 11 km (never your exact position), and only if you share a location;
  • the lunar day and cycle length used for the comparison;
  • if you choose: mood, energy and calm on a 1 to 5 scale;
  • if you did one: the name of the practice done before observing again.

We also store: a random account ID linked to your browser, the date you gave consent, and your confirmation that you're 18 or older. We do not ask for your name or email to join.

Why: to publish aggregate statistics testing what the Svarodaya texts claim about the breath against real observations, and to show you your own history.

What's public: only totals and percentages. Nothing is shown until at least five people have contributed, and any hour with fewer than five logs is hidden, so no individual's pattern can be picked out. Your individual logs are never published.

Where it's kept: in a database hosted by Supabase (supabase.com). Only you can read your own logs through the site.

How long: until you delete it. If the project ever closes, the individual logs will be deleted.

3. Your rights

When you join the open record, the Swar Yoga page shows you an anonymous record code (eight letters and numbers). Because we never ask who you are, this code is the only way to find your data, so please keep a note of it.

  • Get a copy of your data: email adiyogistudios@gmail.com with your record code.
  • Delete your data: email us with your record code. We'll delete your observations, your consent and your account ID. Deleting also withdraws your consent.
  • Stop contributing: choose Stop contributing on the Swar Yoga page. New observations from that browser won't be saved. Anything already saved stays unless you ask us to delete it.
  • Lost your code? If you've cleared your browser or changed device, we can't link a record back to you. Write to us anyway and we'll help where we can.

We'll reply to data requests as soon as we can, and within 30 days.

What we never do

Sell your data, share it with advertisers, track you across other websites, or knowingly collect anything from anyone under 18.

This is not medical advice

The Swar Yoga tool records what you observe and compares it with traditional texts. It does not diagnose, treat or assess any health condition. If you feel unwell, speak to a doctor.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that affects what we collect, the version and date above will change, and we'll ask for your consent again before collecting anything new.
