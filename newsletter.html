Questions

Before you sign up

How often will you email me?

Only when something is published, which is usually two to four times a month.

Why do I need to confirm?

After you sign up, Kit sends a short email with a confirmation link. That stops anyone from adding your address without asking you. If it hasn't arrived, check your spam or Promotions folder.

How do I leave?

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you're off the list.

What happens to my email address?

It's kept by Kit (kit.com), the service that sends our emails, and used only for this newsletter. It is never sold or shared. See the privacy notice.

Can I follow along without email?

Yes. Add the RSS feed to any feed reader, or follow us on Instagram.
